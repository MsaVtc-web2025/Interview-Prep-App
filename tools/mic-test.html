Known limitation: if a student genuinely says the same word twice in a row ("very very important") and that word falls on the seam between two sessions, one "very" is dropped. At a seam there is no way to tell whether Android resent the word or the student said it again. One word short beats a whole phrase doubled, so this is deliberate.
