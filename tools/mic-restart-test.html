Microphone restart test

Simulating how Android closes the mic session by itself - on a fake clock

Running...
What cannot be fixed: the moment a student finishes a sentence, Android closes the mic session itself and plays a chime. No browser API can suppress that chime, so on a long answer they will hear one "off... on" after every sentence. All the app can do is keep the gap between the two as short as possible (checked here) and never reopen the mic for nothing.
